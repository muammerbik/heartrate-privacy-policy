Privacy Policy – Heart Rate Tracker App

Effective date: November 19, 2026

Developer: Muammer Dev

Contact: muammerdev3@gmail.com

This policy explains what information the Heart Rate Tracker App ("the app") handles, where it is stored and who it is shared with. In short: your health data stays on your device. We do not run servers that receive your health data, we do not show ads, and we do not sell or share your data for marketing.

1. Health and personal data stored on your device

The app stores the information you enter or measure only on your device, in the app's private storage:

  • Measurements: heart rate, heart rate variability (HRV) with derived stress and energy estimates, blood pressure, SpO₂, blood glucose, weight, height/BMI, body temperature and breathing rate
  • Notes, symptoms, daily check-ins, measurement tags and illness periods
  • Medicines, dose times and reminder settings
  • Profiles you create (for example for family members): name, birth year, sex, relationship and an optional profile photo
  • Onboarding answers (your goals) and app settings (units, language, date and time format)

We (the developer) cannot see or access this data. It is not uploaded to our servers or to any third party.

Device backups: Like other app data, this information may be included in your device's own backups (for example iCloud Backup or Google device backup) depending on your device settings. Those backups are handled by Apple or Google under their own privacy policies.

2. Camera

Heart rate is measured with the rear camera and flash (photoplethysmography, PPG). Camera frames are analysed in real time on your device to detect your pulse. Images and video are never saved, uploaded or shared. The camera is also used, only if you choose, to take a profile photo, which is stored on your device.

3. Photos

If you choose a profile photo from your library, the selected image is copied into the app's storage on your device. The app does not access any other photos.

4. Notifications

Reminders and medicine timers are scheduled as local notifications on your device. No notification content is sent to a server.

5. Subscriptions and purchases (RevenueCat)

Premium subscriptions are sold through the Apple App Store and Google Play. Payments are processed by Apple or Google; we never receive your payment card details.

To check whether you have an active subscription, the app uses RevenueCat (RevenueCat, Inc.). For this purpose the following data is sent to RevenueCat:

  • An anonymous app user ID generated from a device identifier (Android ID on Android, identifierForVendor on iOS)
  • Your platform (iOS/Android) and purchase and subscription information (product, purchase date, renewal and expiry status)
  • Technical data collected by the RevenueCat SDK to validate purchases, such as IP address, device model, operating system and app version, and store country/currency

This data is used only to provide and restore your subscription. No health data is sent to RevenueCat. RevenueCat's privacy policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy

6. Data you choose to share

  • PDF reports and sharing: When you export a report or share the app, the file or message is passed to the share option you pick (for example email or a messaging app). What happens next is controlled by you and that service.
  • Contacting support: If you email us, we receive your email address and the content of your message, and use them only to reply to you.
  • Ratings: Rating prompts use the official Apple and Google review features.

7. What we do not do

  • No advertising and no ad SDKs
  • No analytics or tracking SDKs, and no tracking across other companies' apps or websites
  • No selling or renting of personal data
  • Health data is never used for marketing, advertising or data mining

8. Data retention and deletion

  • On your device: You can delete individual readings, or delete a profile together with all of its readings, notes and reminders, inside the app. Uninstalling the app deletes all of its data from your device (copies in your own device backups are managed by you).
  • Subscription data (RevenueCat): This is kept for as long as needed to provide your subscription and to meet legal and tax obligations. You can ask us to delete it by emailing muammerdev3@gmail.com.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or Turkey's KVKK), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, or to object to its processing. Because health data is kept only on your device, you control it directly. For subscription data held by RevenueCat, contact us at muammerdev3@gmail.com and we will respond within 30 days.

10. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and is not intended to be used by children on their own. Parents or guardians may create profiles for their children; that information is stored only on the parent's device.

11. Security

Your data is stored in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot access, and is protected by your device's security features (such as your screen lock). Data sent to RevenueCat is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS).

12. Medical disclaimer

The app is for general wellness tracking only. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any disease. Camera measurements are estimates. Always consult a doctor before making medical decisions.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version is always available on this page, and the effective date above shows when it was last changed.
